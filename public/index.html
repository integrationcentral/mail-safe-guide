IF SOMETHING NEEDS ATTENTION

Fix it in the right order.

1

What sends email as your domain?

Pick every tool that puts your domain after the @ in its From address. Your normal inbox is a sender too.

If a real sender is missing here, enforcement can block it later. List everything before moving to quarantine or reject.
💡 Find the website where DNS is managed. It may be GoDaddy, Cloudflare, Squarespace, Wix, Namecheap, or another domain host. This is where you will paste records.
2

Give every sender its ID

In each sending tool, open its domain authentication area. It will generate unique DNS records for you. Keep that page open, open your DNS in another tab, and copy each Name and Value exactly.

Never create a second SPF record. A domain may have only one. If your tool asks for another SPF TXT record, its instructions must be merged into the existing SPF—or use the tool’s automatic authentication. DKIM records usually have unique names and can coexist.
MX is separate: it routes incoming mail to your inbox provider. Do not change working MX records when authenticating an outbound campaign platform unless its official instructions explicitly require it.
3

Add the “don’t pretend to be me” rule

DMARC tells receiving mail systems what to do when a message claims to be from you but fails its ID checks. Add one TXT record with the name below.

TXT
_dmarc
v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:dmarc@yourcompany.com
📬 Create the report address first, or use a trusted DMARC reporting service. Reports can be hard to read; that is normal. The important part is discovering every real service that sends as your domain.
Do not jump to “reject” today. First make sure SPF and DKIM pass for Google/Microsoft, newsletters, invoices, forms, CRMs, and every other real sender.
4

Turn protection up—carefully

DNS changes can take minutes or, sometimes, up to 72 hours. Re-run the checker. When reports show your real senders pass aligned SPF or DKIM, move through these current DMARC phases.

1 · Monitorp=noneCollect reports and find every real sender.
2 · Test enforcementp=quarantine; t=yTest while receivers use special handling.
3 · Enforcep=quarantineRemove testing after legitimate mail is clean. Consider reject when appropriate.
Before enforcement: send tests from every tool you use, confirm they arrive, and review DMARC reports. Keep a rollback copy of your last working record.
Want your logo in some inboxes? That is called BIMI. It comes later and generally requires an enforced DMARC policy. Get mail authentication healthy first; a logo is not the emergency.
